The contract suite checks every row of an `output.csv`: header, 518 sorted
unique rows, JSON validity, seven fields per step, consecutive numbering,
allowed (phase, operation, state) triples, canonical order, status-specific
rules (NOT_APPLICABLE ⇒ `[]`, COMPLETE ⇒ verification only with evidence,
INCOMPLETE ⇒ cited findings and uncited future steps), finding ⇒ remediation

cited id exists and was available at the case's `audit_at`. It also checks the
policy contract: `DELETE_RECORD` never on `audit_record_*`, `legal_hold_*`,
`backup_*`, `tombstone_*`, a SUPPORT_MESSAGE or an ANALYTICS_PROFILE (category
from the run's traces) unless the trace records a passed retention date, and
no `cache_*` or `email_delivery_*` resource anywhere (those exist only in
`system_activity.csv`). 0 policy violations on the 518-case output, the
samples prediction and the answer key.

## 6.2 Investigator agent (L2)

The gate sends a case to the agent on three conditions only: a resolved
subject with zero citable observations; one resource in conflicting states in
evidence of the same capture; a violation supported only by a held
screenshot row. It runs on `claude-opus-5-5`, six turns at most, five strict
tools, and chooses between decisions the engine generated.

**Episode.** As first merged, the agent could choose any engine candidate.
On the 9 gated cases (6 evaluation, 3 samples) it improved none and chose a
less safe reading on 4: `fact_links_only` on samples 01 and 02 (dropping the
L5 link that is the only tie between the account and the person, at 1.000
precision), and the ABSENT side of a same-file conflict on 135 and 383. The
guards reverted all 4 — contract regeneration on 01/02 (the choice left
COMPLETE with no steps), COMPLETE downgrade on 135/383 (the cited file shows
the record PRESENT) — although they were not built for that.

**Change.** The action space is monotone toward caution: a candidate is
admissible only if it keeps every finding and every resource of the engine
default and never turns INCOMPLETE into COMPLETE; candidates that switch a
measured rule off (scope rule, L5) or a rejected one on (L6) are not
generated; a conflict offers only its safer side (spec §6.5: when it cannot
be resolved, the safer reading wins). The agent's one way to add a candidate
is `apply_policy` on a record the parsers missed: the resource id must appear
in an eligible file the agent read, together with one of the subject's
identifiers, and the guards accept that citation only through the verified
record kept in the trace. Tests: `tests/test_agent_monotone.py` (the four
cases have no admissible alternative; looser submissions are refused).

## 7. Known limitations

- 17 of 111 sample gold resources cannot be linked from public data (section 4).
- needs_review rows: **`case_61`** (evaluation) and **`case_04`** (samples) are
  `COMPLETE` with `[]` and flagged `NEEDS_REVIEW`; they are the only rows the
  contract suite reports (`complete_empty`). The guards keep them on purpose
  (section 2.1); the L2 agent will re-examine them. On case_04 the answer
  key's steps do re-derive from their cited evidence: the evidence is there,
  the subject's accounts are what our linking cannot reach.
- 504 of 518 evaluation subjects are observed in exactly one eligible
  evidence file (3 in two, 11 in none; `--suite coverage`): a single misread
  file decides a case. The late-file check shows the effect: removing
  `evidence_92` flips four sample cases.
- 12 vision_only screenshot rows are held until the agent re-reads their files;
  5 belong to `case_45`.
- L6 is off. The agent has changed no row: on every gated case it either had
  no admissible alternative or kept the engine decision.
- `case_527`: ticket attachment `support_3896` is treated as a support
  message (REDACT) rather than an attachment (DELETE), because a later file
  classifies the same resource as a support message. Which classification
  should win is ambiguous; no sample covers it, so it is left as is.
